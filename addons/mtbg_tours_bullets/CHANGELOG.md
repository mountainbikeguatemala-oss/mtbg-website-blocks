# Changelog

Every version of this extension, newest first. Tags: `git tag -l 'mtbg_tours_bullets-*'`.

## 19.0.0.1.0 · 2026-10-25

- **Bullets block** (website editor › Text › Bullets): a list whose bullets are icons. Its options: a style preset
  (Check box, Bookmark, Cross, None), or an icon from the icon picker with its colour, size, box (colour, size,
  corners) and the space after it. Wrapped lines hang under the text; right-to-left languages put the icon on the
  right. Works in pages, tour descriptions and reusable blocks. Listed under Custom blocks on the Extensions page.
- The old site's lists keep their look with no change to the content: `.highlights` (bookmark), `.checkbullet`
  (check box) and `.xbullet` (cross).
